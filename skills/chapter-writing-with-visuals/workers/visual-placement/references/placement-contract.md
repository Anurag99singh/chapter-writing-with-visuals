# Visual placement contract

Chapter Writing owns story development, prose, Anshul style, formatting and internal humanizing. Visual Placement performs none of those passes again.

Create separate Artifact B by faithfully copying passing Artifact A reader-facing content and native formatting. Preserve every chapter word, heading, paragraph boundary, approved section order, icon, bullet, emphasis run, evidence attribution, citation, number, caveat and fixed punchline. Remove only named workflow appendices and execution audit material.

Insert only exact human-KEEP assets from the current Unified Visual Review. Use the mapped subsection and place a figure immediately after the paragraph explaining its relevant idea, at an existing paragraph boundary. Never separate a claim from its supporting explanation. If the mapped position is ambiguous, use the closest supported paragraph boundary within that subsection and record the reason separately; do not rewrite or move paragraphs to accommodate it.

For `GEN-* / GENERATED_EXPLAINER`, use the exact generated asset reviewed and kept by the human. Never rerun Adapt/Render, regenerate, edit, restyle, reinterpret or substitute it. A GEN asset explains already-cited evidence and must not be represented as an evidence source or replace canonical citations.

Add sequential figure numbers, concise evidence-faithful captions, appropriate source/evidence attribution and accessibility descriptions. Preserve image proportions, scale for readability and adjust only local figure spacing or page breaks needed for placement. Never crop away evidence or change chapter typography, bold, italics, bullets or headings.

No fresh research, replacement images, prose/style/humanizer pass or new preview labels. Honor EXCLUDE. Any current GEN PENDING blocks execution. Account for exact-asset failures with a specific reason in the separate report, without substitutes.

Verify B against A after removing named appendices and accounting for figure/caption/accessibility additions. Reader prose and native formatting must match. Inspect figure placement, legibility, caption/attribution association, exact approved-asset identity and ordering. Writing/emphasis feedback returns to Chapter Writing; image-placement/layout/caption feedback can be addressed here.

Standalone boundary: only GEN-* / GENERATED_EXPLAINER candidates are valid. No source extraction is available. Review and asset hashes must bind to current passing Artifact A and approved evidence.
