# Coverage ledger

Use this ledger for long sources, source batches, slide or photo sequences, videos, podcasts, articles with several sections, and PDFs.

## Source units

Create one row per meaningful unit:

| Unit ID | Location | Source meaning | Distinct value | Disposition | Post ID | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Location may be a photo filename, slide number, timestamp, section heading, page, paragraph, figure, chart, or note identifier.

Disposition must be one of:

- `Standalone post`
- `Series post`
- `Supporting evidence`
- `Already covered`
- `Exact duplicate`
- `Unreadable`
- `Logistical/personal`
- `Out of scope`

For `Supporting evidence` or `Already covered`, name the exact post and the claim supported. Never use these labels as a general consolidation bucket.

## Distinct-value test

Keep a unit as a candidate idea when it adds any of the following:

- a mechanism or component
- a stage or optimization method
- a framework, quadrant, or taxonomy
- a concrete example or use case
- a failure mode, boundary, or safeguard
- a trade-off, comparison, or decision rule
- a consequence for users, teams, roles, economics, or strategy
- a quantitative result that changes the interpretation

Topical similarity does not make two units duplicates.

## Series decision

Split an idea into multiple posts when a single image would:

- reduce a framework to unexplained labels
- omit relationships between components
- remove examples needed for understanding
- combine definition, implementation, optimization, and failure handling into generic bullets
- require small text or more than roughly 120 on-image words

Give the series a unifying question or thesis. Each post must still deliver a complete standalone insight.

## Completion check

Before approval, confirm:

1. Every source unit has a disposition.
2. Every retained claim maps back to source evidence.
3. Every excluded unit has a specific reason.
4. Supporting units link to an exact post.
5. No distinct mechanism, example, trade-off, or consequence disappeared during compression.
6. The proposed number of posts emerged from the source rather than a preset quota.
