# Article visual reference profile

Inspect assets/article-references/product-engineer-comparison.png, approved-two-princes.png and approved-institution.png. The leverage examples were approved by Anshul on 3 October 2026.

- Communicate with one visible relationship: equal inputs with different choices; personal bottleneck versus shared coordination; a learning loop; or a concrete comparison.
- Use large actual section-title sentences, simple line drawings, concise annotations and breathing room.
- Prefer around 40–80 words when sufficient; preserve meaning rather than filling a quota. Avoid long prose paragraphs and detailed castle scenery.
- Treat callouts and comparison areas as parts of one argument. Boxes are allowed; generic unrelated cards are not.
- Use landscape 1536 × 1024 for article images unless requested otherwise; omit author identity by default for this mode.
- Use humans for people and robot icons for agents; keep that distinction unambiguous.
- Render all content together. Supply an approved reference to image generation as style guidance when possible.
- Let the article explain nuance while the visual delivers the central realisation. Preserve any caveat whose omission would mislead.
- Vary the mechanism across a series. Do not force every section into a two-column comparison.
