# LinkedIn visual design systems

All systems target a 1080 × 1350 px, 4:5 portrait canvas. Use 64 px horizontal safe margins, 56 px top margin, and 48 px bottom margin.

## Editorial Impact

Reference: `assets/references/editorial-impact.png`

- Palette: paper `#F2E6D6`, ink `#1F201E`, orange `#CE612F`, warm secondary `#C88E6E`, brown `#423C32`, neutral `#A8A39B`.
- Display fonts: Anton, Bebas Neue, or League Gothic.
- Supporting fonts: Roboto Condensed Bold or Oswald SemiBold.
- Body fonts: Inter, Roboto Condensed, or Source Sans 3.
- Sizes: display 72–94 px; secondary heading 36–48 px; labels 28–34 px; explanatory body 30–38 px; footer or signature metadata 18–21 px.
- Surface: warm paper grain, black keylines, selective distressed edges.
- Visual treatment: editorial collage, utilitarian circular icons, documentary or conceptual imagery, orange reserved for consequential emphasis.
- Do not copy the reference's panoramic strip or three-column structure by default.

## Data Signal

Reference: `assets/references/data-signal.png`

- Palette: navy `#022C45`, signal blue `#0374FC`, pale blue `#86B7FD`, mid blue `#4688CE`, muted blue `#274D68`, light text `#F4F8FF`.
- Headline fonts: Poppins SemiBold, Aptos Display Bold, or Inter Bold.
- Body fonts: Inter, Roboto, or Source Sans 3.
- Numeric fonts: Inter SemiBold or IBM Plex Sans Medium with tabular figures.
- Sizes: headline 52–66 px; subtitle 32–38 px; labels 28–34 px; explanatory body 30–38 px; numeric emphasis 28–36 px; footnote or signature metadata 18–21 px.
- Surface: flat navy with restrained blue wave or geometric accent.
- Visual treatment: precise alignment, pale-blue information marks, calm spacing, almost no decorative effects.
- Do not assume the content requires bars or ranking.

## Neon Intelligence

Reference: `assets/references/neon-intelligence.png`

- Palette: near-black `#040419`, deep violet `#251355`, electric purple `#8C47DB`, mid violet `#5A29A0`, soft white `#F4F1F6`, muted text `#A0A2A7`, optional cyan `#41C8FF`.
- Hook fonts: League Spartan ExtraBold, Montserrat ExtraBold, or Archivo Black.
- Body fonts: Inter, Manrope, or Aptos.
- Sizes: hook 66–88 px; highlighted phrase 82–106 px; explanatory support 30–38 px; labels 28–34 px; author name 29–34 px.
- Surface: cinematic near-black field with selective ultraviolet glow.
- Visual treatment: one luminous focal illustration, thin neon lines, geometric nodes, strong negative space.
- Do not copy the reference's left-text/right-diagram arrangement by default.

## Visual Notebook

Reference: `assets/references/visual-notebook.png`

- Palette: paper `#FEFCF9`, soft paper `#F6F3EE`, marker black `#252525`, blue `#2767D8`, green `#25834C`, purple `#7550B8`, yellow `#F2C94C`, lavender wash `#E8E2F3`.
- Headline fonts: Kalam Bold, Patrick Hand, or Comic Neue Bold.
- Annotation fonts: Kalam Regular or Patrick Hand.
- Signature tag: Inter Medium.
- Sizes: headline 56–72 px; highlighted phrase 60–78 px; section heading 32–40 px; explanatory body 30–38 px; annotation or diagram label 28–32 px; author name 28–34 px.
- Surface: warm off-white paper with subtle marker washes.
- Visual treatment: mature hand lettering, confident imperfect arrows, sparse diagrams, restrained doodles, two or three accents per post.
- Do not copy the reference's top-to-bottom narrative or its footer-box layout by default.

## Shared signature

- Name: `Anshul Jain`
- Tag: `AI & Gen AI Product Mgmt`
- Design the name, tag, and empty circular portrait slot with the post from the beginning.
- Keep the signature visually subordinate to the idea but legible on a phone.

- Keep the signature on the post's continuous surface. Do not add a generic card, pill, badge, or contrasting footer strip unless that treatment already belongs to the composition.
- **Editorial Impact:** use condensed editorial typography, an ink or orange keyline around the empty slot, and a restrained rule or label treatment that feels printed with the post. Tuck it into a border, collage edge, or share a lower band with the takeaway; continue paper texture, documentary imagery, or rule work through the neighbouring space.

- **Neon Intelligence:** use soft-white type and a restrained violet or cyan ring whose glow matches the post. Connect it to an existing circuit line, node, frame, or callout; avoid a separate luminous signature panel or empty lower row.
- **Visual Notebook:** use mature hand lettering for the name, clear sans-serif text for the tag, and an imperfect marker-drawn circular outline integrated into the whiteboard surface. Place it beside a takeaway, inside unused diagram space, or in a natural corner without extending the canvas narrative.
